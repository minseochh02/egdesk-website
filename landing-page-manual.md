# Welcome to EGDesk

EGDesk is a desktop workspace for running the practical side of a business: money, websites, data, content, research, automation, and AI assistance. Instead of jumping between browser tabs, spreadsheets, admin tools, and separate AI chats, EGDesk gives each job its own workspace and keeps them connected.

The home screen is simple on purpose. Each card opens a focused tool. Start with the part of your work that matters most today, then add more as your team grows into the system.

## Why People Use EGDesk

EGDesk is built for teams that need to move faster without losing control of their data and workflows.

- Bring finance records, tax documents, imported files, and company research into one place.
- Edit, host, and improve websites with AI-assisted workflows.
- Turn repeated browser or desktop tasks into reusable automations.
- Create blog and social content from the same brand direction.
- Connect AI models to real business tools, files, and knowledge.
- Keep sensitive workflows on the desktop when local control matters.

## Start with the Work You Already Do

### For finance and admin teams

Open **Finance Hub** when you want EGDesk to help with the money side of the business.

Finance Hub is designed around real business records, not just dashboards. It supports bank and card transaction workflows, HomeTax-related records, electronic tax invoices, bills, business registrations, and client records. You can review data in tables, filter it, export it, and prepare it for accounting or internal review.

Use it when you need to:

- Gather bank and card transactions.
- Import transaction Excel files.
- Review deposits, withdrawals, card spending, balances, and account-level records.
- Work with HomeTax sessions, business numbers, electronic tax invoices, bills, and client data.
- Export finance data to spreadsheets.

Best first action: open **Finance Hub**, then start with the bank, card, or HomeTax area that matches your current records.

### For teams managing websites

Open **Coding** when you want to work on a website or homepage project with AI help. Open **Hosting** when you are ready to serve or manage that project.

The website workflow is meant to cover more than editing a page. EGDesk can help you manage projects, chat with an AI assistant about changes, view local and hosted addresses, push work toward hosting, and keep history or backups around website edits.

Use it when you need to:

- Update a business homepage.
- Ask AI to help edit a project.
- Preview a site locally.
- Move a project toward hosting.
- Keep project history and backups.
- Improve the website with SEO and SSL checks.

Best first action: open **Coding** for editing, then use **SEO-Analyzer**, **SSL-Checker**, and **Hosting** as the site gets closer to launch.

### For operators working with spreadsheets and files

Open **My DB** when your work starts in Excel, CSV, SQL, downloaded files, or repeated data imports.

My DB turns business files into structured tables that can be browsed, searched, exported, backed up, and synced. It supports development and production-style environments, which helps teams test data workflows before pushing them forward.

Use it when you need to:

- Import Excel or CSV files.
- Import or export SQL.
- View tables and inspect rows.
- Sync browser downloads or desktop downloads into tables.
- Back up and restore data.
- Push prepared data from development toward production.

Best first action: open **My DB**, import one spreadsheet, and confirm the table looks right before adding sync or automation.

### For marketing and content teams

Open **Business Identity**, **Blogging**, and **Social Media** when you want EGDesk to help turn business knowledge into content.

Business Identity helps organize brand direction and internal knowledge. Blogging supports writing, planning, and scheduling longer-form content. Social Media focuses on creating and scheduling social posts. Together, they make a practical content pipeline: define the business, write the message, publish it in the right format.

Use it when you need to:

- Clarify brand positioning and messaging.
- Keep internal knowledge in a reusable place.
- Draft blog content with AI assistance.
- Schedule content.
- Create social posts from the same business direction.
- Support customer channels such as Kakao Channel.

Best first action: open **Business Identity** before generating content, so the rest of the tools have clearer business context.

### For research and sales preparation

Open **Company Research** when you need to understand a business before a call, proposal, partnership, or review.

Company Research can start from a website or just a company name. It is built for AI-powered company intelligence: finding the company, building a structured view, keeping past research, and supporting email-based sharing when connected.

Use it when you need to:

- Research a company from its website.
- Start research from a company name only.
- Review executive, detailed, and summary-style outputs.
- Re-run research when information changes.
- Keep a history of researched companies.
- Support client risk review workflows.

Best first action: paste a company website or company name, run research, then save or share the result.

## Automation Without Starting from Code

EGDesk includes automation tools for the work people usually repeat by hand.

### Browser Recorder

Use **Browser Recorder** when the repeated task happens in a browser.

It can record a web flow from a URL, generate Playwright-style test code, show code as it records, preserve downloaded files, chain a download into a later upload step, save tests, import/export tests, replay them, and schedule replays.

Use it for:

- Website testing.
- Repeated portal workflows.
- Download-and-upload chains.
- Browser tasks that need a saved script.
- Scheduled browser checks.

### Desktop Recorder

Use **Desktop Recorder** when the repeated task happens in a desktop app or system window.

It is designed for recording desktop interactions and turning them into workflow scripts. On macOS, it may require accessibility permissions because it needs to observe and control desktop actions.

Use it for:

- Desktop app workflows.
- Repeated local software operations.
- File-handling routines.
- Automation that cannot be done in a browser.

### Rookie

Use **Rookie** when the goal is structured extraction and export.

Rookie is the right place when you know the outcome you want, such as extracting data from a workflow and exporting it cleanly.

## AI That Can Work with Your Tools

EGDesk is not only an AI chat box. Several areas are built around connecting AI to business systems.

### AI Center

Use **AI Center** as the general hub for AI workflows and agents. It is the place to think about AI as an operating layer across the app.

### MCP Server

Use **MCP Server** when AI needs access to tools or data. MCP Server supports running and managing tool connections, including services such as Gmail, Google Apps Script-style project access, My DB queries, finance-related data, and other connected workflows.

Use it when you want AI to:

- Query imported business tables.
- Work with connected services.
- Access tool-backed workflows instead of only answering from memory.
- Run through a managed local or shared server setup.

### Ollama

Use **Ollama** when you want to run local AI models on your machine. This is useful when privacy, offline work, experimentation, or local control matters.

### PageIndex

Use **PageIndex** when your knowledge is locked inside PDFs.

PageIndex indexes PDFs into hierarchical document trees and lets AI retrieve information by structure. It is useful for manuals, contracts, reports, policies, legal material, and dense reference documents.

Use it when you need to:

- Index a PDF.
- View its document structure.
- Ask questions against indexed documents.
- Retrieve answers with page-aware context.

### Voice Transcript

Use **Voice Transcript** for voice-driven workflows that need structured responses and connected knowledge. It can combine voice interaction with JSON-style outputs and retrieval from tools such as legal sources or indexed PDFs.

## Tools That Support the Whole System

### API Key Management

Many AI and connected-service features need provider keys. **API Key Management** is where you add, test, activate, deactivate, edit, or remove those keys.

Start here if an AI feature cannot run because a provider key is missing.

### Gmail

Use **Gmail** when your workflow depends on Google Workspace mailbox access. EGDesk includes Gmail browsing and export-oriented workflows for domain email data.

### Docker

Use **Docker** when your team needs to control containers from inside EGDesk.

### Desktop Control

Use **Desktop Control** for desktop environment and remote access settings.

### OpenClaw

Use **OpenClaw** for account creation and token-related workflows connected to a Google profile.

### Lawyer and Neuron

Use **Lawyer** for AI-assisted legal review and management. Use **Neuron** for neural analysis and pattern-recognition style workflows.

## A Simple First-Week Plan

### Day 1: Get oriented

Open the home screen and choose only one main area:

- Finance team: **Finance Hub**
- Website team: **Coding**
- Data team: **My DB**
- Marketing team: **Business Identity**
- Research or sales team: **Company Research**

Add API keys only if the feature asks for them.

### Day 2: Bring in real data

Import one real spreadsheet, connect one finance source, index one PDF, or run one company research job. Keep it small and verify the result.

### Day 3: Make the result useful

Export a report, save a table, draft content, update a website page, or share a research result. The goal is to complete one real workflow end to end.

### Day 4: Add AI assistance

Use AI where it helps with a specific task: writing, research, website edits, document retrieval, or tool-connected workflows.

### Day 5: Automate one repeated task

Use **Browser Recorder**, **Desktop Recorder**, or **Rookie** for something your team repeats often. Start with a low-risk task, replay it, and confirm the output before relying on it.

## What Makes EGDesk Different

EGDesk is for teams that need AI to sit close to actual work. It does not treat finance, websites, files, content, research, and automation as separate worlds. A spreadsheet can become a database table. A browser task can become a replayable workflow. A PDF can become searchable knowledge. A website can be edited, checked, and hosted from the same desktop environment.

That is the value of the home screen: it is not just a menu. It is a map of the work EGDesk can help you finish.
